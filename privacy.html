Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what the GullyDRS app does with your information.

Your videos stay on your phone

GullyDRS processes your cricket videos on your phone. Videos are never uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see them. Replays the app creates are saved on your phone. You decide whether to share them.

Information we don’t collect

GullyDRS has no account and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, your contacts or your location.

Settings stored on your phone

The app saves simple settings on your phone, such as your batter’s hand and your monthly review count. These settings stay on your phone.

Feedback and support emails

The Feedback and Contact forms in the app open your own email app, addressed to gullydrs@gmail.com. We use what you send only to reply to you.

For a “wrong result” report, you can choose to attach the review video, a picture of the stumps and the result. We use these only to reply to you and to improve ball tracking. We delete them when you ask us to.

Purchases (Google Play)

The Pro plan will be sold as a subscription through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We never see your card details.

Advertising

After launch, the free version will show ads from Google AdMob. AdMob uses an advertising ID on your phone to show ads. To learn how Google uses information for ads, read Google’s ads policies.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings. Open Settings, then Google, then Ads. Menu names can vary a little by phone.

Crash reports

Google Play may share anonymous crash reports with us. These help us find and fix problems in the app.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera – to record deliveries.
  • Photos and videos – to open a video you choose and save the replay.

People in your videos

Videos may show players and passers-by. Please only record people who are happy to be filmed, and ask a parent or guardian before filming children. Videos stay on your phone, so we do not store them, unless you choose to email one to us in a feedback report.

Children

GullyDRS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Clubs and leagues

Clubs and leagues may use an optional cloud processing option. This applies only under a separate written agreement with us.

Your choices and deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Replays you saved to your photos or videos stay there until you delete them.

To delete any feedback emails you sent us, email gullydrs@gmail.com and we will delete them.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers. Its text font (Inter) is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to download it.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, an optional cloud feature), we will update this page and the date above before the change goes live.

Contact

Questions about privacy: gullydrs@gmail.com
