Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what the GullyDRS app does with your information.

Your videos stay on your phone

GullyDRS analyses cricket videos directly on your device. Videos you record or choose are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see them. Replays the app creates are saved on your phone, and you decide whether to share them.

What we collect

The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, contacts or location.

If you choose to send us feedback or join the test group by email, we receive your email address and the message you send, and use it only to reply to you.

Permissions the app uses

  • Camera – to record deliveries.
  • Photos and videos – to open a video you choose and save the replay.

People in your videos

Videos may show players and passers-by. Please only record people who are happy to be filmed, and ask a parent or guardian before filming children. Because videos never leave your phone, we do not store or process them.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, an optional cloud feature), we will update this page and the date above before the change goes live.
