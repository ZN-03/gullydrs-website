How it works

  1. Set up the phone

    On a tripod or held still behind the bowler, in line with the stumps.

  2. Record the delivery

    Start before the run-up ends, stop after the ball reaches the batter.

  3. Get the decision

    See the ball’s path, where it pitched, where it hit, and whether it was going on to the stumps.
